# Plan: #474, the i.MX 95 M7 console back on LPUART7

Owner ruling 1243 (2026-11-24), #474 option (a): "LPUART7 for the imx95 M7
console." The FRDM-i.MX95 console port is LPUART7 on GPIO_IO36/37 at ALT2
(PR #449 bench record, sections 14 and 15); with LPUART3 the image is silent
there. Main has LPUART3 at 0x42570000 on interrupt 64 and no pad request.

The code comes back by hand from PR #449's head (commit 3d88b103 for the
LPUART7 values, 5519a305 for the `_PAD` names); #449 is not merged or
cherry-picked.

## Slices, in order

1. Failing test first: test_imx95_hal gains
   test_the_console_is_lpuart7_at_0x42690000_on_interrupt_68 and the NVIC
   bit 4 (interrupt 68) expectations; the model decodes the LPUART at
   0x42690000.
2. The instance move: soc_regs.h (TFW_IMX95_LPUART7_BASE/IRQ and a
   build-time assert), hal_uart.c (tfw_cm_irq68), hal_early.c comment,
   imx95_entry.c, imx95_model.h and board_profile.h comments. test_imx95_hal
   passes.
3. The pad request: SCMI pin-control builder (scmi.h/.c), the shared-memory
   CRC-32 (scmi_mu.h/.c), console_pins.h/.c, hsm_port.c's channel, the
   board's SCMI channel (MU5_MUA) and pads (GPIO_IO36/37, ALT2), hal_uart.c
   calling tfw_imx95_console_route() before the LPUART reset.
   Tests: test_imx95_console_pins (#449's seven plus
   test_the_board_routes_gpio_io36_37_at_alt2), the route stub and its
   order check in test_imx95_hal; mk/host.mk builds and runs it.
4. Docs: docs/boards/frdm-imx95-m7.md, docs/multi-arch/imx95-design.md,
   changelog.d/474-imx95-m7-lpuart7.md.

## Out of scope

- docs/SPEC.md and ROADMAP.md (the SPEC PR, row-563-q24, lands first).
- #449's imx-boot packager, IMX_BOOT, step marks, REG_DUMP and tx pump.
- The SCMI domain ids (still open).
