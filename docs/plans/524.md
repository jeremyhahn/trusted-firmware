# Plan: #524 the xkms adapter reads the claims through sdk/go

## Objective

The xkms adapter (`xkms/avr/attestation.go`) takes the alias certificate's
claims from go-xkms's `dice.Verify` alone, while `sdk/go` owns the claim
format (`sdk/go/appevidence.go`): two decoders that can drift. Device-onboarding
audit #522, slice 2, open question 4 of `docs/device-onboarding/architecture.md`.

`xkms/go.mod` pins `sdk/go` v0.4.2-alpha, and that tag exports `AliasUCCS` and
`ApplicationEvidence` (checked with `go doc` on the module cache copy), so the
brief's "yes" branch applies: no sdk/go tag is needed.

## Slices

1. **Claim 273 decoded by sdk/go** (no hardware).
   - `attestation.go`: after `dice.Verify` accepts the chain,
     `checkAliasCertificate` reads the UCCS with `tfw.AliasUCCS` and the
     measurement with `tfw.ApplicationEvidence`, and reports it as
     `Attestation.ApplicationEvidence`. A claim 273 that sdk/go refuses
     refuses the attestation with sdk/go's `*tfw.EvidenceError`.
   - `attestation_test.go`: the real-device vector yields its 32-byte
     measurement; the synthetic alias certificates carry a well-formed claim
     273; a missing claim 273, a hash that is not 32 bytes, and a file that is
     not "app" are refused with `tfw.ErrBadFormat`.
   - `docs/device-onboarding/architecture.md`: open question 4 closed.
   - `changelog.d/524-xkms-sdk-claims.md`.

## Tests

`make xkms-test` (with `GOTOOLCHAIN=go1.27.4` in the lane container, whose
local Go is 1.27.3); `make ci` from a clean tree.

## Out of scope

- A new sdk/go tag or any go.mod version bump.
- go-xkms's own reading of claim 273 at finalize (open question 3).
- The firmware's encoding of the claims.
