# Plan: row 540, Ethernet transport on the STM32H573I-DK

## Objective

The STM32H573I-DK brings its link up, takes a DHCP lease on the dev network
and carries tfw-wire frames over TCP to the bench host, verified on the board
(ruling 768's completion plan). The tree has no Ethernet driver and no IP stack
for `stm32h5`, and the onboarding ADR defers the Ethernet port, so the stack
choice goes to the owner first as an ADR:
[`../multi-arch/adr-stm32h5-ethernet.md`](../multi-arch/adr-stm32h5-ethernet.md).

## Slices, in order

1. **The ADR (this slice, docs only).** Options (a) lwIP over our own MAC and
   DMA-descriptor driver, (b) lwIP over ST's HAL ETH driver, (c) NetX Duo,
   (d) an IP stack of our own; a recommendation; the PHY and the RMII pins as
   far as they can be stated without UM3143; how the frames ride TCP; the open
   questions. The board page links it, and a changelog entry records it.
   Tests: none; the repo's docs checks.
2. **Driver and DHCP, built in CI** (after the owner rules on the ADR). The
   chosen stack pinned and composed, the MAC/DMA and PHY driver behind
   `src/hal/cortex-m/stm32h5/`, the `include/tfw/eth.h` socket and DHCP calls
   over it, the board's `ETH=1 DHCP=1` row built in CI. Tests: host unit tests
   of the descriptor ring and the PHY register sequence against a register
   model, the `tfw_eth_*` seam against a loopback netif, and the flash and RAM
   figures of the row against the board's budget.
3. **The hardware run** (after #535). Board
   record first, then the image flashed; link up, a DHCP lease, and the
   cross-implementation attestation rows over TCP from the bench host.
   Evidence on the board page, with no address, host name or device path.

## Out of scope

- Any code, `mk/` file, board profile or `docs/SPEC.md` change in slice 1.
- TLS on the device; the allow-list and the frames do not change.
- The secure world (onboarding ADR open question 1) and the NUCLEO-H563ZI.
- The Wi-Fi module on the board.
