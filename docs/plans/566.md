# Plan: row 566, CI builds the CRYPTO=stm32h5 image (Closes #566)

## Objective

`make firmware BOARD=stm32h573i-dk CRYPTO=stm32h5` failed on a missing include
(`soc_regs.h`) that no CI job caught: CI built the STM32H573I-DK at
`CRYPTO=soft` only. Found in the #544 hardware check (PR #557, ruling 768).

1. The include. `src/crypto/stm32h5/provider.c` includes `soc_regs.h` by its
   own name; `mk/firmware.mk` gives `provider.o` `-I$(HAL_DIR)/stm32h5`
   (`src/hal/cortex-m/stm32h5`), landed with #557. Every
   `src/crypto/stm32h5/*.c` preprocesses with the firmware include paths, so
   no source change.
2. CI. A step in the cortex-m job after the `CRYPTO=soft` STM32H573I-DK
   firmware step: `make firmware BOARD=stm32h573i-dk CRYPTO=stm32h5`
   (toolchain image), same `if:` and `scripts/in-toolchain-image.sh` form.
   The job's toolchain image compiles and links it on every PR; actionlint
   passes. No `runs-on` change (ruling 999).
