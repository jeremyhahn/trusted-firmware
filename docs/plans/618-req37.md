# #618, requirement 37: the 2560 sheds OTA, never the verifier

Objective: the OTA audit in `docs/ota/engineering.md` names a test for each of
requirements 28, 37 and 45 (its slices 7 and 8), and requirement 37 gets the
guard it lacked. Ruling 1052 / DS-9745.

## Slices

1. `scripts/test_boot_shed_ota.py` (run by `make scripts-test`): on the soft
   tier `make bootloader BOARD=atmega2560` refuses `BOOT_OTA=1 BOOT_STORE=0`
   and `BOOT_OTA=0 BOOT_STORE=1` at parse time, the refusal offers
   `atmega2560:soft:soft:p256-soft:0:0` and no soft OTA or store row, and the
   default soft row resolves to `:0:0` and passes the gate. It passes on main;
   adding `atmega2560:soft:soft:p256-soft:1:0` to `BOOT_SUPPORTED` fails it.
2. `docs/ota/engineering.md`: rows 28, 37 and 45 name their tests and are
   done; slices 7 and 8 are marked done.
   - 28: `test/unit/test_ota_auth_mutate.c:
     test_the_element_session_and_ota_auth_are_each_needed_to_activate`
   - 37: the two cases of slice 1
   - 45: `test/unit/test_esp_ota.c:
     test_a_confirm_before_the_deadline_keeps_the_image`,
     `test_no_confirm_at_the_deadline_resets_into_the_rollback`

## Out of scope

- `mk/boot.mk` is not changed: the rows are already right on main.
- Slice 6 of #618 (the ESP32 confirm verifies its signature) waits on the
  owner, on its own branch; #618 stays open.
