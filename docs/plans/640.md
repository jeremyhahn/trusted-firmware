## Slice: boot OTA drip (branch `row/640-boot-drift-batch`, into #759)

What broke: `make integration-test-boot`'s simavr case "a drip of valid OTA
frames during a session" (`test_valid_frame_drip_hits_the_ceiling`) got no
OTA_BEGIN reply on the ATmega2560 OTA image.

Cause: the session's empty pass drifted from the 77 cycles
`boot/hal/avr/boot_tick.h` (`TFW_BOOT_POLL_CYCLES`) counts on. avr-gcc kept
`serve()`'s `total_polls` in registers, the pass fell to about 64 cycles and
the 10 s idle window closed at about 8.3 s, before the drip's frame at 8 s.

Fix: `total_polls` is volatile on AVR and on the host (which mirrors the
328P's figures), so on AVR it lives in SRAM and the pass costs 77 cycles
whatever the rest of `serve()` compiles to. The qualifier comes from
`TFW_BOOT_POLL_COUNTER_T` in each `boot/hal/<arch>/boot_tick.h`, found on
`$(BOOT_HAL_DIR)` with no `#if`; the host unit-test rules that compile
`recovery_ota.c` gain `-Iboot/hal/host`. On Cortex-M it is plain `uint32_t`,
the loop main compiles (Codex round 4, P1). One changelog fragment,
`changelog.d/640-boot-empty-pass.md`.

Tests: the drip case answers its frame and the session still ends at the
ceiling, not before 80% of it and no later than the ceiling plus the verify;
the idle case closes not before 80% of the configured idle time. Gate:
`make integration-test-boot`.

Codex round 4, P2, in the same slice: the Go SDK's `Decode` refuses the
LEN-67 request the C framer refuses (`ErrBadFormat`), pinned by the shared
vector `TFWV_FRAME_PING_OVERSIZE_REQ` / `FramePingOversizeReq` in
`test/unit/test_frame.c` and `sdk/go/codec_test.go`.

Open, not fixed here: `boot/hal/cortex-m/boot_tick.h`'s 20-cycle figure is
still unmeasured, and so is the Cortex-M counter's type.
